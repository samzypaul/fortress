Who We Are

Pioneering Biomedical Engineering Solutions in Malawi

Founded in 2018, Fortress Medical Engineers (FME) Limited was established to address the critical need for reliable, high-quality, and professional medical device support, ensuring patient safety and operational efficiency across the region.

Our Mission

To provide unparalleled biomedical engineering services, ensuring medical equipment is installed, commissioned, maintained, and repaired to the highest international standards of safety and performance.

  • Maximize Equipment Uptime
  • Enhance Patient Safety
  • Reduce Operational Costs

Our Vision

To be the leading and most trusted biomedical engineering partner in the Region, renowned for our technical excellence, ethical practice, and commitment to human life.

  • Regional Expansion & Leadership
  • Continuous Training & Certification
  • Innovating Asset Management Solutions
Our Foundation

Principles That Define Our Work

Integrity

Upholding the highest ethical standards in all dealings, from servicing quotes to spare parts sourcing.

Precision

Committing to calibrated accuracy and meticulous attention to detail in every service we offer maintenance task and repair.

Collaboration

Our Journey

A Brief History of FME

1
January 2018: Company Formation

FME Limited is officially incorporated, recognizing the local market demand for highly specialized, independent BME support.

2
2019-2021: Securing Key Contracts

FME Limited secures its first major Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMCs) with prominent private hospitals, solidifying its reputation for reliability and rapid response.

3
2022: Expanding Regional Footprint

Successfully executed commissioning and installation projects in neighboring Southern African countries, proving FME’s capacity for cross-border operations.

4
Present: Focus on Future Technology

Continuing to invest in certified training for complex, high-tech diagnostic and therapeutic equipment, positioning FME for future healthcare advancements.
