**lesson 2 what is a prompt**

1. Define what a prompt is and explain its role in mercor rubric projects
2. understand the importance of applying your professional expertise to create prompts that exposes **AI model failure points**
3. **use the iterative testing loop to surface models weaknesses effectively**



A prompt is the input you give to an ai model the question , instruction or task that tells the model what you want it to do

.The goal is to find prompts that test the limits of the AI models reasoning and capabilities in ur professional domain.

. you will know you have a created a strong prompt when the ai model fails to provide a strong response that means you have exposed a failure point.



**iterative testing loop**

 **prompt--> AI model response--> if the model response does not answer effectively--> move onto creating a golden response + rubric**

               **if the model answer effectively --> modify the original prompt or create entirely new prompt**



**lesson 3 : requirement for a strong prompt**



**objectives**

1. identify the qualities that define a high quality prompt
2. recognize common weakness in the prompt and how they should be avoided
3. apply constrain margins of error and clear deliverable specification to strengthen your prompts



qualities of a good prompt

1. require deep reasoning
2. &nbsp;be representative of the actual work in your profession : the prompt should mirror how professionals approach problems in your field and it should require the expertise of someone in your profession in order to properly answer the question.
3. have a clear objective: the prompt should be well defined so that any seasoned professional expert with the right resources provided would know exactly what deliverable is expected, including the required format , length and any other constraints.
4. include necessary constraints: specify any conditions limitations or perspective the model should adopt 
5. have a solution that expert in the field would generally  agree on ( or that the quality of the answer can be judged by objective criteria.: if the  answer to the prompt is highly subjective and could vary from person to person then its not a great prompt, instead they should be a clear basis for what constitutes a correct or superior answer.
6. 6\. have clear margin of error

guidance  for determining if a prompt is too open ended or subjective

i. expert agreement

ii. bounded scope

iii. evaluation anchors

iv. dependency on taste
